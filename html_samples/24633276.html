Fully furnished dream family house, exclusive location !

Fully furnished apartment.

Welcome to your perfect fully furnished family nest in one of Helsinki's sunniest, most exclusive location !

Because your family deserves to live in beautiful surroundings, this contemporary detached house is ready to move in – just bring your own personal belongings and start living.

House: 3 bedrooms + office (or flexible 4th bedroom) Fully equipped kitchen with dining table, cutlery, cooking equipement, beds throughout the house, TV, internet (included) and ping pong table for family fun. Private sauna, laundry room and 2 bathrooms. 3 toilets in total. A bright, modern floor plan designed for comfortable everyday life.

Location Advantages: Only 500 m to the beach (4 different beaches to choose from!) and pristine natural forest. Supermarket 5 minutes away. Marina and golf 5 minutes away. Tennis, football and basketball 10 minutes away. Close to kindergarten and schools a short walk or bike ride away in a family friendly neighborhood. A safe area for your children.

Connections: 20 min by car to the centre of Helsinki (Central Railway Station) 30-40 minutes by metro, bus or bicycle. 30 minutes to the airport by car. 36 min to Keilalahti/Tapiola/ Microsoft HQ by car.

Rental period: Available until the end of June 2027. = No rent to pay during your summer holiday. It is possible to continue living in the house until 2028/29 with a new agreement.

A pre-made home that combines nature, comfort, and a high standard of living — perfect for a family expat or moving family who wants to settle in easily.

RENT: 3700 euros/month. No additional fees (Electricity and water charges, city fees, internet are included in the rent.)
