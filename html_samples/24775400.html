Welcome to this newly renovated premium one-bedroom apartment in the district of Taka-Töölö, Helsinki. This apartment is appointed with quality materials and contemporary furnishing elements, providing a timeless Scandinavian flair. Having a large twin bed and free Wi-Fi, this apartment is suitable for two guests to live in perfect comfort for a longer stay.

The apartment includes a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances and all the amenities you need: tableware, pots and pans, oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, coffee machine and a kettle. The light and airy bathroom is even equipped with its own washing machine. There is a large array of shops and restaurants near the apartment, from age-old brick-and-mortar stores to daily services. You will also find a plethora of parks in the close vicinity, which the surrounding area of Töölö is well known for, such as Piilopuisto and Kirjailijanpuisto. The shoreline is also located just a stone’s throw away, where you are able to find excellent running and walking paths.

Furthermore, access to public transportation is great, as numerous bus and tram lines run along Topeliuksenkatu and Mannerheimintie to and from city center, making commuting a breeze. Please, don’t forget to enjoy the amenities this timeless early 20th century building provides: you will find, for example, a sauna, rentable bicycles and excellent public spaces. For motorists, parking is also available on the property for an extra charge.
