Luxury Townhouse in Espoo / Kauklahti – Spa, Sauna & Yard

Looking for a fully furnished, move-in-ready home with space, comfort, and premium features? This luxury townhouse is located in Kauklahti, Espoo — offering a peaceful residential environment with easy access to services and connections. The home is architect-designed and features a yard, great views, and high-end amenities including a spa area, sauna, and an all-around Sonos sound system. A projector and cinema screen add to the experience, creating a comfortable and elevated living space for longer stays.

Etsitkö täysin kalustettua ja muuttovalmista kotia, jossa on tilaa, mukavuutta ja premium-varustelu?
