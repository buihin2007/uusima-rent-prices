As Oy Helsingin Merilokki sijaitsee Herttoniemenrannassa lähellä merenrantaa. Kohteen läheisyydestä löytyvät hyvät ulkoilumaastot sekä Tuorinniemen uimaranta. Kauppakeskus Hertsiin on matkaa hieman alle kilometrin verran ja noin kilometrin päässä sijaitsee myös Herttoniemen metroasema.

Vuokralainen tekee itse sähkösopimuksen ja sitoutuu ottamaan laajan kotivakuutuksen sekä vastuuvakuutuksen. As Oy Helsingin Merilokki koostuu hissillisestä viisikerroksisesta AB-talosta sekä erillisestä C-talosta. AB-talon asukkaiden yhteiskäytössä on talopesula, ja jokaiselle asunnolle kuuluu irtaimistovarasto. Kiinteistö on täysin savuton, ja tupakointi on kielletty huoneistojen parvekkeiden ja piha-alueiden lisäksi myös taloyhtiön piha-alueella. Kiinteistö on siirtynyt energiatehokkaaseen maalämpöön keväällä 2026. Oikeudet muutoksiin pidätetään. ------------------- The materials were selected by the interior architecture firm Silvana with respect for the building’s original character. As part of the renovation, the kitchen and bathroom, including its fixtures and fittings, have been renewed, the flooring has been replaced with light-coloured plank laminate, and the walls have been repainted. Comfort and interior upgrades will be carried out in the apartments throughout 2026, one staircase at a time. The apartments in Staircase A will be completed in October and will be available for rent from 9 October 2026. The apartments in Staircase B will be completed in November and will be available for rent from 6 November 2026.

- Security deposit equivalent to one month’s rent - Water fee €27/person/month (fixed, not adjusted) - Pets are welcome - Rent includes DNA 10M internet connection - Parking spaces available for rent separately - Fixed-term lease agreement with the earliest possible termination date 12 months after the start of the lease. After this, the agreement will continue until further notice. If the tenant terminates the agreement before the earliest possible termination date, a fee equivalent to two month's rent will be charged.

As Oy Helsingin Merilokki is located in Herttoniemenranta, close to the seafront. The surrounding area offers excellent opportunities for outdoor activities, and Tuorinniemi Beach is nearby. Hertsi Shopping Centre is just under one kilometre away, and Herttoniemi Metro Station is approximately one kilometre from the property.

The tenant is responsible for arranging their own electricity contract and is required to maintain comprehensive home insurance as well as liability insurance. As Oy Helsingin Merilokki consists of a five-storey AB building with an elevator and a separate C building. Residents of the AB building have access to a shared laundry room, and each apartment has its own storage unit. The property is completely smoke-free. Smoking is prohibited on apartment balconies and private outdoor areas, as well as throughout the housing company’s grounds. The property switched to energy-efficient geothermal heating in spring 2026. The right to make changes is reserved.
